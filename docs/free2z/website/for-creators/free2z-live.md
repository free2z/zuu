# Host a livestream

Bring people into the moment. Host a public conversation, meet your subscribers, offer a paid performance, or invite a small group with a private link.

## Before you start

Hosting requires at least **150 2Z usage credits**. Check your credit balance before choosing a room. Check the price and access controls shown in the app before starting.

1. Open the stream controls from your dashboard or creator tools.
2. Choose a stream type. For pay-per-view, review the usage-credit price per minute.
3. Test your microphone and camera; allow browser access when prompted.
4. Start the room and share its link. Keep a private invitation link private.
5. Use the room's leave/end controls to end the session for everyone when finished.

| Access type | What viewers need | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public broadcast | Public room link | Host pays the displayed platform usage cost |
| Subscriber access | Current membership | Members use their subscription access |
| Pay-per-view | Credits at the displayed price | Qualifying delivered access earns USD under your accepted creator agreement and service terms |
| Private invitation | Secret invitation link | Access follows the room's displayed controls |

Before offering compensated pay-per-view, complete payee verification, accept the creator agreement and service terms, and confirm Credits & Earnings shows you are ready for creator compensation. [Learn about creator earnings](/revenue-sharing/).
