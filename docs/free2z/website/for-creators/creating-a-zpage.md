# Publish a zPage

A zPage is your space for a story, a guide, or an idea worth sharing. Sign in, open the editor, and make something yours.

1. Open the editor from Write, your dashboard, or My Pages.
2. Add a title and your content. Use the toolbar or `/` menu for headings, lists, media, and other blocks.
3. Add an optional cover and subtitle.
4. Open page settings to check the public link name, categories, and access settings.
5. Preview the page, then select Publish. Reopen it from My Pages to edit and Update it later.

Keep a copy of important writing. Check the save status before leaving, especially if your connection drops.

Subscriber-only pages give members access under your membership settings. Subscriptions use platform credits; they do not create USD creator earnings.
