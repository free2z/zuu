# Boost a page

Give a page you love a little lift. A boost uses 2Z credits to help a published zPage get noticed.

1. Open a published page.
2. Open its action controls and choose Boost.
3. Check your usage balance and the displayed cost before continuing.
4. Check the result before retrying after a connection problem.

You can boost your own or another person's page. Credits spent on boosts are platform usage, not USD creator compensation.
