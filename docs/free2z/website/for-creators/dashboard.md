# Creator Dashboard

The dashboard is your control center after you sign in.

Open it from your **picture** (top right) → **Profile**, or from the creator tools links in the app.

## What you can do

### Pages

- See drafts and published zPages  
- Search your titles  
- Create a new page  
- Jump into the editor to revise  

### Profile

Edit what the public sees: display name, avatar, cover, bio, and Zcash address.

### Media

Upload and manage files for pages and profile images.

### Stream

Start Free2Z Live and manage your stream settings.

### Notifications

Activity about your content and account.

### Credits & Earnings

Check your usage credits, purchase results, memberships, and separate USD earnings. Credits & Earnings shows your setup steps and payment requests. [Explore Credits & Earnings](/guides/money/billing).

### Settings

Account preferences and security (password, email, two-step verification if you use it).

## Tips

- Publish regularly — fresh, boosted work is easier to discover  
- Add a Zcash address early so supporters can tip right away  
- Use drafts freely; publish when the title and cover feel strong
