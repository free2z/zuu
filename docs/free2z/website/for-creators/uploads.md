# Upload media

Give your story a cover, add a photo, or share a useful file. Upload media from your account or editor; the app shows supported formats and size limits. Holding more 2Zs unlocks larger uploads.

1. Choose a file you have the right to publish.
2. Review the displayed size, format, and usage limits.
3. Wait for the upload to finish and copy its link.
4. Add that link to your page, then preview it before publishing.

Treat a public upload link as public. Do not upload identity documents or payment evidence as public media. Follow the account verification or support instructions for submitting these documents.
