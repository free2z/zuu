# Your ideas. Your people.

Free2Z is a place to make something, find your people, and keep the conversation going. Publish a story, share a quick thought, host a live room, or build a useful app. Your audience can read, reply, join, and support you—all without an advertising feed getting between you.

## Make a home for your work

A [zPage](/features/publishing) can be a polished essay, a practical guide, or a first brave attempt. Add images and media, translate it for more readers, and gather your work on a profile that feels like you.

Prefer a quick exchange? [Converse](/features/converse) gives small thoughts and replies a place to grow. Prefer a live moment? [Bring people into a room](/features/live) for a talk, a workshop, or a performance.

## A little support goes a long way

Share [Tuzis](/getting-started/tuzis) to help someone use Free2Z, subscribe for their membership benefits, or send [Zcash directly](/privacy/zcash) to their wallet. Qualifying delivered paid livestreams and approved SDK contributions can earn [USD compensation](/revenue-sharing/) from 2Z Inc.

## Build something useful

Have an app idea? Explore the [open-source SDK preview](/for-creators/sdk-applications). Free2Z provides user-authorized AI access and credit spending; you build and host the experience.

## Free to be you

**We will never sell your personal data.** Read public pages without an account, choose whether to add an email when you join, and decide what to share publicly. We care deeply about accessibility and want more people to feel at home here.
