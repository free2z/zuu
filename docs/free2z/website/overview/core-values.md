# Free to be you

Your ideas deserve room to grow. Free2Z brings people together to create, share, learn, and support one another—without an advertising feed getting between you.

## Create something that matters

Tell a story. Show someone how. Start a conversation. Build a tool you wish existed. There is room here for the polished project and the first brave attempt.

## Privacy is part of freedom

**We will never sell your personal data.** You can browse public pages without an account, and email is optional when you sign up. Choose what you share publicly and explore [Zcash’s shielded privacy options](/privacy/zcash) for direct support.

## Support good work

A thoughtful comment, a shared link, a few Tuzis, a membership—small acts can keep a creator going. [Usage credits](/getting-started/tuzis) make platform tools accessible; [creator earnings](/revenue-sharing/) pay for qualifying delivered services. Clear prices help you decide where to put your support.

## Make room for everyone

We care deeply about accessibility. Reading, writing, and connecting should be welcoming experiences, whatever tools you use. If something gets in your way, [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/free2z/zuu/issues). Tell us what you were trying to do, and we’ll try to fix it.

## Build with care

Respect other people and their work. Ask good questions, credit your sources, and help someone take their next step. We keep improving Free2Z with the people who use it—one useful idea at a time.
