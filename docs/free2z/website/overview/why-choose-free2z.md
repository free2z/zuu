# Why Free2Z?

## Your work, your people

Share an idea and find the people who care about it. Publish a zPage, host a live conversation, or build an app. Give your audience a place to return to and a way to support what you make.

## More connection, fewer distractions

Free2Z is ad-free. Small platform credit charges fund useful tools and help reduce spam, so there is more room for writing, discovery, and conversation.

## Support that does something

Share Tuzis to help someone use the platform. Subscribe for membership benefits. Send Zcash directly to a creator’s wallet. Qualifying paid livestreams and SDK contributions can also earn real USD compensation from 2Z Inc.

[Explore ways to support creators](/for-supporters/) or [learn about creator earnings](/revenue-sharing/).

## Privacy you can choose

**We will never sell your personal data.** Browse public pages without signing up, choose whether to add an email, and use shielded Zcash where your wallet supports it. Your profile and published work are yours to shape.

## Start small. Make it yours.

Write a first page. Try a new idea on camera. Share something that made you think. You don’t need a Zcash wallet to publish—just a story worth telling.

[Get started](/getting-started/).
