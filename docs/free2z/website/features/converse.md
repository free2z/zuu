# Small posts, good conversations

Not every idea needs a whole article. [Converse](https://free2z.com/converse) is Free2Z’s microblog: share a quick thought, ask a question, and reply to people who catch your interest.

## Say something

Browse the conversation, then sign in when you want to join. Write a short post or reply, follow a thread, and explore what’s new or getting attention. Check the displayed 2Z cost before posting.

## Let an idea grow

A quick observation can become a longer zPage. Share its link in the conversation, invite a response, and keep building on what you learn.
