# A page for your ideas

Write an essay, publish a tutorial, tell a story, or collect the links you keep sending your friends. A zPage gives your work a title, a home, and a link worth sharing.

## Start with a thought

Open **Write**, add a title, and get your idea down. Headings, lists, links, images, and embeds help you make it readable. Preview your page, then publish when you’re ready. Come back to edit as your thinking grows.

Your profile gathers your published work in one place. You can also offer subscriber-only pages through your membership settings.

## Reach readers in more languages

Use the editor’s translation tools to prepare another language version. Review the translation, give it the same care as the original, and publish it for readers to choose. AI translation uses 2Z credits; check the displayed cost before starting.

## Give your story a visual side

Add a cover, a photo, or other supported media. Your account’s 2Z balance determines upload size limits, so a larger balance opens room for larger files.
