# Share a page or profile

## Share a zPage

1. Open the article  
2. Tap **+** (bottom-right) → **Share**  
3. Choose:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy link** | Copies the page link |
| **X** | Opens share on X |
| **Reddit** | Opens share on Reddit |
| **LinkedIn** | Opens share on LinkedIn |
| **Telegram** | Opens share on Telegram |

## Share a profile

On a creator’s profile:

1. Click the **share** control  
2. Copy the profile link or share through the options shown  

## Tips

- Share after you publish  
- A [boost](/guides/support/boost) plus a share can help more people find the page
