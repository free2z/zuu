# Share 2Zs

Give someone more room to create. Sharing 2Zs helps them use AI, livestreams, comments, and other Free2Z features. Shared credits stay usage credits; they aren’t USD earnings.

1. Open another creator’s profile and choose **Support**.
2. Choose the **2Z** usage-credit option.
3. Check the recipient, amount, and your available credits.
4. Confirm the displayed sharing action.
5. Check the result before retrying after a connection problem.

You need to sign in to share credits. If you need more, [buy credits on the Credits & Earnings page](/guides/money/buy-2z).

Direct Zcash support is a separate option that sends funds from your wallet to the creator's address. [Read that guide](/guides/support/donate-zcash) before sending.
