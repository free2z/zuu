# Send Zcash support

Like what someone is making? Send Zcash straight from your wallet to theirs.

## Open the support panel

On the creator’s profile or zPage, choose **Support → Zcash**. The panel shows the recipient’s address and a QR code. A zPage can use its own support address, so check the destination for the page you’re supporting.

## Make it personal

Enter the amount you want to send. If the receiving address supports memos, add a message or edit the suggested one. The recipient can read it; include only what you want to share.

Open your wallet with the payment link, scan the QR code, or copy the address. Before sending, check the address, amount, memo, and network fee in your wallet. Payment-link and memo support vary between wallets.

Prefer a shielded receiver when it’s available and your wallet supports it. Your wallet shows the transaction’s privacy details.

## No address yet?

The panel tells you if the creator hasn’t added a Zcash address. You can [share 2Z credits](/guides/support/donate-2z), or come back when they’ve set up their wallet.

Creators: add your address in [Edit profile](/guides/settings/edit-profile), or set a page-specific address in the zPage editor.
