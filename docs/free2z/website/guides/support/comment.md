# Comment on a zPage

Comments use a small amount of **2Zs**. The default is **1** 2Z per comment.

## Steps

1. Open a zPage and scroll to **Comments**  
2. Sign in if you aren’t already  
3. Fill in:
   - **Headline** (optional, up to about 100 characters)  
   - **Content** (your comment, up to about 1000 characters)  
   - **Weight / Tuzis** — how many 2Zs to put on this comment (default **1**)  
4. Optionally tap **Preview** to check how it looks  
5. Click **Post**  
6. You’ll see a **Comment posted!** message  

## Replies

Click **Reply** under a comment to answer someone in the thread.

## Votes

Signed-in readers can **upvote** or **downvote** comments.

## Why comments cost 2Zs

A small cost helps cut down spam. Putting more 2Zs on a comment can show stronger support.
