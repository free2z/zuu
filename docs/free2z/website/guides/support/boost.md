# Boost a zPage

Boosting helps a page get noticed on Free2Z. Each boost spends **2 2Zs**.

## Steps

1. Open a published zPage  
2. Tap the floating **+** button (bottom-right)  
3. Tap the **lightning / boost** button  
4. If you’re signed out, log in and try again  
5. On success:
   - The score under the button goes up  
   - You’ll see a short **Thanks for the boost!** message  

If you click very quickly, Free2Z may ignore extra clicks for a moment so boosts aren’t double-counted by accident.

## If something goes wrong

- Not enough 2Zs — top up in [Credits & Earnings](/guides/money/buy-2z) and try again
- An error message appears — wait a moment and retry  

## Tips

- Boost after you publish, with a strong title and cover  
- You can boost other people’s pages, not only your own
