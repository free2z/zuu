# Create an account

You can create a Free2Z account with a username, password, and the required CAPTCHA check. Email is optional, but we recommend it so you can recover your account later.

## What you’ll need

- A username (this becomes your public name on Free2Z)
- A password of **at least 8 characters**
- A moment to complete a quick “I’m not a robot” check when asked

## Steps

### 1. Open sign in

1. Go to [free2z.com](https://free2z.com).
2. In the top-right corner, click **Sign In** (or **Sign In / Sign Up**).

### 2. Switch to Sign up

In the window that opens, choose **Sign up** (or a link like “Need an account?”).

### 3. Choose a username

1. Enter a **Username**.
2. Keep it under **128** characters.
3. This is how people will find you.

### 4. Create a password

1. Enter a password (**at least 8 characters**).
2. Enter the same password again under **Confirm password**.
3. Use the eye icon if you want to double-check what you typed.
4. Strength tips may appear (length, letters, numbers, symbols).

::: warning Add an email if you can
If you skip email and forget your password, Free2Z cannot look it up for you. Add email if you want reset options later.
:::

### 5. Optional: add email

1. Fill in **Email** if you want recovery and account messages.
2. After signup, Free2Z may ask you to check your inbox and confirm the address.

### 6. Accept terms and submit

1. Signup means you agree to the [terms of service](/legal/terms-of-service).
2. Click **Sign up**.
3. Complete the required “I’m not a robot” check.

### 7. Optional: sign up with X

Under **More login options**, you can choose **Sign up with X** and finish on X’s site, then return to Free2Z.

## After you sign up

- Your picture menu appears in the top bar
- You can write pages, comment, tip, subscribe, and buy 2Zs
- Check **Credits & Earnings** for your balance after signing in

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Robot check failed | Try again, or refresh the page |
| Username taken or invalid | Pick another name |
| Passwords don’t match | Re-enter the confirm field |
| Stuck on email check | Open the confirmation link from your inbox, then continue |
