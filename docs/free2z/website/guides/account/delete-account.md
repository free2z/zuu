# Delete your account

## Where

**[free2z.cash/account/delete](https://free2z.cash/account/delete)**, also linked from **Your picture → Settings → Account** and from **Connected apps**.

No email address is needed.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the account you want to delete
2. Read what the account will lose
3. Type your username
4. Choose **Delete account**

If your last sign-in was more than five minutes ago, the page asks you to sign in again first, with whichever method you use.

Finish or cancel any purchase in progress first.

Your account is hidden right away and deleted 30 days later. Signing in before then asks whether to keep it; keeping it cancels the deletion. Your profile, email, sign-in methods, pages, uploads and connected-app access are deleted, and you are signed out everywhere. Payment, 2Z ledger and tax records are kept without your profile, as law and accounting require; remaining 2Z credits can no longer be used. Your username stays reserved for 30 days after the deletion, then anyone can use it. Content under review after a report is kept hidden until the review ends.

[Full details](https://free2z.cash/docs/privacy-and-security/delete-account)
