# Log in

## Steps

### 1. Open sign in

Any of these work:

- Click **Sign In** in the top bar
- Try something that needs an account (like writing a page) — you’ll be asked to log in
- Open **Credits & Earnings** while signed out — you’ll be asked to log in first

### 2. Enter your details

1. **Username**
2. **Password**
3. Click **Log in**

You may see a quick “I’m not a robot” check.

### 3. If you turned on two-step verification

1. A second step asks for a code
2. Open your authenticator app on your phone
3. Enter the **6-digit code**
4. Click **Verify**

You can go **Back** if you need to re-enter your password.

### 4. You’re in

The top bar shows:

- Your **picture** (opens the account menu)
- A **notifications** bell (on larger screens)
- A **Live** indicator when you’re streaming

## Your picture menu

| Item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your creator home / dashboard |
| **Settings** | Account and profile settings |
| **Credits** | 2Z balance and purchases |
| **Contribute** | Free2Z on GitHub (external) |
| **Log out** | Signs you out |

## Log out

1. Open your picture menu  
2. Click **Log out**

## Also available
