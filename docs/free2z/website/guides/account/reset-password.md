# Reset password

Password reset needs an **email address** on the account.

## Steps

1. Open **Sign In**  
2. Click **Forgot your password?**  
3. Enter the email on the account  
4. Click **Send Reset Link**  
5. Open your inbox and follow the link Free2Z sends  
6. Choose a new password when asked  

## After reset

Return to [Log in](/guides/account/log-in) with your username and new password.

::: tip
If you never added an email, reset may not be possible. Add email under [Settings → Account](/guides/settings/email-and-theme) while you still have access.
:::
