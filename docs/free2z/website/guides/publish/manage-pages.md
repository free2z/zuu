# Manage your pages

**My Pages** is where you find all your drafts and published zPages.

You can also manage them from your **public profile** when you’re signed in as the owner (**Published** and **Drafts** tabs).

## Open My Pages

1. Sign in  
2. Open your creator tools / dashboard  
3. Choose **Pages** (or **My Pages**)

You can also leave the editor with **X** — that usually takes you back to your pages list.

## What you can do

### Create

Click **Create New Page** to open the editor.

### Go live

Click **Go Live** when you want to start a stream.

### Search your pages

1. Type in **Search pages…**  
2. Press Enter  
3. Matching pages appear in the list  

### Change the layout

Switch between **card** and **list** views.

### Open or edit

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **View** | Opens the public page (if it’s published) |
| **Edit** / pencil | Opens the editor for that page |

### Browse longer lists

If you have many pages, use the page numbers at the bottom to move through them.

## On your public profile (as owner)

When you view your own profile:

1. **Published** — pages people can read  
2. **Drafts** — pages still private to you  
3. Search box to find a title  
4. Sort by newest, oldest, or popular  
5. Filter by tags  
6. **New Article** to start writing  

## Draft vs published

| State | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Mostly just you — not listed publicly |
| **Published** | On your profile and in search (when a custom link name is set) |
