# Write and publish a zPage

A zPage is your article or post on Free2Z. You write it in the editor, then publish when you’re ready.

You need to be signed in.

## Create a new page

### 1. Open the editor

Any of these work:

- Click **Write** or **Create** in the app
- From your dashboard, click **Create New Article** or **Create New Page**
- On your own public profile, click **New Article**
- From **My Pages**, click **Create New Page**

### 2. Add a title

Click the title field and type a clear title. You need a title before you can save or publish.

Free2Z can suggest a short link name from your title. You can change it later in settings.

### 3. Optional: cover image

1. Click **Add Cover** at the top
2. Paste an image link
3. When the link works, you’ll see a preview

### 4. Optional: subtitle

1. Click **Add Subtitle**
2. Write a short summary — this shows on cards and at the top of your page

### 5. Write your content

1. Type in the main writing area
2. Type **/** for quick inserts (headings, lists, images, tables, and more)
3. Use the toolbar if you prefer buttons over typing

Your work saves automatically a couple of seconds after you stop typing (once there’s enough to save).

### 6. Page settings (gear icon)

Open the settings panel for extra options:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom link name** | The short name in your public page link |
| **Categories** | Topics like Art, Education, Music, and more |
| **Zcash address** | Optional donation address just for this page |
| **Subscribers only** | Only people who subscribe to you can read it |

::: warning Custom link name
If you leave the custom link name empty, your page may not show on your profile or in search. Set one before you promote the page.
:::

### 7. Preview

Switch how you work:

| Mode | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Write** | Just the editor |
| **Split** | Editor and preview side by side |
| **Preview** | How readers will see it |

### 8. Publish

1. Make sure you have a title and some content
2. Click **Publish**
3. Later edits use **Update** instead of Publish

You’ll see a small status note while it saves (**Saving…**, **Saved**, or an error).

### 9. Leave the editor

- Click **X** at the top to go back to **My Pages**
- Or open your public profile to see the live page after it’s published

## Edit an existing page

1. Open **My Pages** or your public profile
2. Click **Edit** (or the pencil) on the page
3. Make your changes and click **Update**

Only you can edit your own pages.

## Before you share it

- [ ] Strong title and cover
- [ ] Custom link name set (so people can find it)
- [ ] Categories chosen
- [ ] Published (not still a draft)
- [ ] Optional: [boost](/guides/support/boost) or share for more reach
