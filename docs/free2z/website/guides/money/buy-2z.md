# Buy 2Zs

Pick up some Tuzis for your next AI request, livestream, membership, or boost. Holding more credits also unlocks larger uploads. **2Zs are platform usage credits**: you can use and share them on Free2Z, but cannot cash out a balance.

Choose **Credits** from your account menu, choose a quantity, and select an available payment option. Review the price and fees before paying.

Shortcut: open [free2z.cash/buy-2z](https://free2z.cash/buy-2z) to go straight to buying, then choose Zcash for the recommended option.

## Pay with Zcash (recommended)

Zcash is the recommended way to buy 2Z: payments are shielded, and Free2Z charges **no fee and no spread** on Zcash purchases.

### 1. Review the estimate

Credits & Earnings shows an estimated Zcash amount and credit quantity, along with the payment destination and memo. It uses a conservative **10% estimate buffer**, so you will likely receive more credits than the estimate. That buffer is not a deduction or a guaranteed minimum: the market price can move before your payment is processed.

Free2Z calculates the credits from the actual Zcash received after confirmation using a fresh market price when it processes the payment. **Free2Z currently charges no Zcash purchase fee or spread.** Your wallet’s network fee is separate. The estimate does not reserve an exchange rate.

If Credits & Earnings shows a bonus offer, read its terms before paying. An offer must be active and eligible when your confirmed payment is processed. The highest eligible offer applies; offers do not stack. An unknown or expired code does not prevent normal credits or another eligible offer.

### 2. Copy the destination and memo

In your Zcash wallet, copy the **destination and memo** displayed by Credits & Earnings. Choose the amount you want to send and check all the details before paying. The memo helps match the payment to your account; leaving it out or changing it can require support to resolve the payment.

### 3. Wait for confirmations

Once you send, allow time for the payment to reach Free2Z and receive confirmations. **Do not send again just because credits have not appeared.** Check Credits & Earnings for the purchase result.

### 4. Check the credits applied

The result shows the credits calculated from the received payment. Some purchases repay an outstanding usage charge as well as adding spendable credits; check the displayed breakdown.

If the result is unclear, keep the transaction reference and contact [help@free2z.com](mailto:help@free2z.com). A received payment awaiting resolution requires delivery or an applicable refund.

## Pay by card

Card purchases add a fee of 10% or $0.50, whichever is larger.

1. Choose the card option and review the quantity and checkout total.
2. Complete the secure card checkout.
3. Return to Credits & Earnings and check the purchase status.

Keep the payment receipt. If the result is uncertain, check the original purchase or ask support before starting another payment.
