# Credits & Earnings

Choose **Credits** from your account menu to open **Credits & Earnings**, where you can check your credits, purchase results, memberships, and creator earnings.

## Your usage credits

Your available 2Zs pay for services on Free2Z. Buy more using an available card or Zcash checkout, or use them for the next thing you want to do.

Under **Credit activity**, open **Card purchases** to see how each card purchase was applied. A proven purchase total may include repayment of an outstanding usage charge as well as credits added to your spendable balance. Read the breakdown rather than treating the full total as extra spending money. This list covers card purchases; Zcash purchases, credit sharing, and service charges are not included.

If a purchase is processing or awaiting resolution, keep its reference and check for updates. Don’t pay again simply because credits have not appeared. Contact [help@free2z.com](mailto:help@free2z.com) for delivery or refund help.

## Memberships

Check your subscriptions, renewal prices, and access dates. Cancel renewal when you no longer want the membership. Subscriptions use platform credits and provide the creator’s membership benefits.

## Creator earnings

The earnings panel records USD owed by 2Z Inc. for qualifying delivered pay-per-view livestreams and SDK services. It shows the amount owed, reserved for payment requests, and available to request. Credits, gifts, and memberships are separate from these earnings.

Complete payee verification and read and accept the creator agreement and service terms before offering compensated services. Credits & Earnings shows any remaining setup steps. If a balance is unavailable, wait for it to load before deciding what to request.

## Request a creator payment

Enter an available USD amount and your authorized shielded Zcash destination. Review the method before confirming: conversion at the external market spot rate when the company signs the payment, without a company exchange spread. The company pays network fees and upward rounding.

A request reserves the amount while payment review takes place. Follow the status through to confirmation. If a response is uncertain, keep the original request reference and retry that request rather than creating a second payment.
