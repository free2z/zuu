# Use the home feed

The home page is where you discover stories and creators.

## What you’ll see

1. **Featured story** — a large highlighted zPage  
2. **Trending** — pages picking up interest  
3. **Popular zPages** — widely read posts  
4. **Creator spotlight** — people you might like  
5. **Topics** — themes and categories  
6. **Get started** prompts — for new visitors and creators  

## How to use it

### Open a story

Click a card’s title, cover, or read link.

### Open a creator

Click the author’s name or picture on a card.

### Search from home

1. Use the search field in the top bar (“Find something…”)  
2. Press **Enter**  

### Sign in without leaving

Use **Sign In** in the top bar anytime.

## With or without an account

| Action | Without account | Signed in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read public pages | Yes | Yes |
| Search / browse creators | Yes | Yes |
| Boost / comment / tip 2Zs | No | Yes |
| Send Zcash support | Yes (from your wallet) | Yes |
