# Read a zPage

A zPage is a creator’s article or post. Open one from home, search, a profile, or a shared link.

## What’s on the page

1. **Back** — return toward home  
2. **Cover and title** — with the author’s name and picture  
3. **Description** — short summary, if the author added one  
4. **Body** — the full post (text, images, embeds, and more)  
5. **Comments** — discussion at the bottom  
6. **Floating + button** — quick actions (bottom-right)  

## Author

Click the author’s name or picture to open their profile.

## Quick actions (+)

Tap **+** to expand:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Boost** | Spends **2 2Zs** to support and lift the page (sign-in required) |
| **Support → Zcash** | Shows the creator’s Zcash QR/address (if they set one) |
| **Share** | Copy the link, or share to X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Telegram |

## Members-only pages

If a page is **Subscribers only**, you may need to [subscribe](/guides/support/subscribe) before you can read the full post.

## Outside content

Some pages include videos or embeds from other sites. Free2Z may ask before loading them, because that can share some browser information with that site. Choose **Load once**, **Load & remember**, or always allow when prompted.
