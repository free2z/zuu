# Browse creators

Find people to read, tip, or subscribe to.

## Steps

1. Open **Creators** from the app (or a Creators link on home)  
2. Scroll through the list of creator cards  
3. Click a card to open that person’s profile  

## Filters

Open the filter panel (side panel on desktop, slide-up on mobile). You can often filter by:

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Find a name or handle |
| **Verified** | Verified, not verified, or all |
| **Membership** | Free vs paid membership |
| **Zcash only** | Creators who accept Zcash tips |
| **Min posts** | At least this many published pages |
| **Min followers** | At least this many fans |
| **Sort** | For example activity or popularity |

A **fan** is someone with a current, unexpired subscription to that creator, so
**Min followers** and the **Most followers** sort both count active subscribers
— not tips, boosts, or page funding. Sort by **Most popular** if what you want
is the creators whose pages have drawn the most support.

Active filters show as removable tags. Use **Clear** to reset.

## Tips

- Prefer **Verified** if you want more established accounts  
- Use **Zcash only** when you want wallet-level privacy options
- Combine search + verified for a tighter list
