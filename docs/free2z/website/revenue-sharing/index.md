# Creator earnings

Turn your livestreams and apps into real earnings. Share a performance, teach something useful, or build an application people love. For qualifying services you deliver, your earnings are **USD owed by 2Z Inc.**, paid to your authorized shielded Zcash address.

Apply, get approved, and accept the terms for your service before offering compensated work. Your payee and contribution must qualify for the service you want to deliver.

People use 2Zs to unlock a paid livestream or use a supported app service. For qualifying services you deliver as an approved creator, Free2Z records your agreed compensation in the separate USD earnings balance.

## What earns compensation?

Two activities qualify under the creator agreement:

- **Paid livestreams:** the qualifying pay-per-view access you deliver to viewers.
- **SDK applications:** an approved, licensed app contribution that delivers a qualifying service through Free2Z, with its agreed customer-facing markup.

Before offering a compensated service, complete payee verification and read and accept the creator agreement and the service’s terms in Credits & Earnings. These describe your contribution, delivery responsibilities, how compensation is calculated, and payment timing. Confirm that Credits & Earnings shows your account is ready for creator compensation, and complete the service’s approval requirements before offering it.

Sharing 2Zs and subscriptions provide platform credits and membership benefits. They do not create USD earnings.

## Credits and earnings in everyday terms

**Credits let you use Free2Z.** Buy them, spend them on platform services, or share them. A credit balance has no cash-out option.

**Earnings are money the company owes you for qualifying delivered services.** Credits & Earnings records them in USD, independently of your credit balance. Receiving a gift of credits or holding an unused balance does not earn money.

## Set up your creator details

1. Open creator verification from your dashboard. There is no minimum credit balance to apply.
2. Provide the requested identity and authority information for the person or organization receiving payment, and complete the eligibility review.
3. Complete any required tax information.
4. Read the full creator agreement and relevant service terms in Credits & Earnings, then accept them if you want to participate.
5. Get approved for your service and follow its setup requirements before offering compensated work.

The app tells you if setup is incomplete or a feature is unavailable. Contact [help@free2z.com](mailto:help@free2z.com) if you need help resolving a requirement. Keep sensitive documents out of public uploads.

## Read your balance

Credits & Earnings shows:

| Amount | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Owed | Recorded USD compensation |
| Reserved | The part committed to a payment request |
| Available | The amount you can request |

If Credits & Earnings cannot load your earnings, wait for it to reconnect before making a payment decision. An unavailable display is not a confirmed balance.

## Request payment

Enter a positive available USD amount and a shielded Zcash address you control. Read the payment details before confirming. The request reserves that amount while the company reviews your payee, address, tax information, and applicable payment restrictions.

The agreed payment method converts the requested USD amount to Zcash at an external market spot rate when the company signs the payment. There is no company exchange-rate spread. **2Z Inc. covers network fees and rounds upward where needed**, so rounding does not reduce the USD amount paid.

There is no dollar minimum. The ordinary contractual due date is **30 calendar days after verified service settlement**, unless your service terms give an earlier date, subject to lawful holds. The company aims to review requests within seven calendar days after the required information is complete. Contact support if your review is delayed.

A request is not a completed payment. Follow its status in Credits & Earnings and keep the original request reference if a response is uncertain. A confirmed payment settles the recorded USD obligation once; it does not redeem your credits. Ending participation does not erase amounts already owed.
