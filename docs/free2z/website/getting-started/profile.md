# Set up your profile

Introduce yourself. Your profile brings your pages, biography, memberships, and optional Zcash support address together in one place.

1. Sign in and open your profile settings from the dashboard.
2. Edit your display name and biography. Choose what you want to make public.
3. Add an avatar or header image if you want one.
4. Save, then open your public profile to check the result.

An optional Zcash address here receives direct wallet-to-wallet support. It is separate from the address you submit for payment of actual company-owed USD. Check that any public support address belongs to you.

Membership prices are in usage credits. Subscriptions share utility credits and provide access benefits; they do not create USD earnings.
