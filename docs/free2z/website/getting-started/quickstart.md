# Start using Free2Z

Browse public pages before creating an account. To publish or use account services, open Free2Z and sign up with a username, password, and the verification shown on screen. Add an email if you want an email recovery option; store your password securely.

1. Set a display name and biography in your profile settings.
2. Open the editor, add a title and content, and publish a zPage.
3. Buy usage credits on the Credits & Earnings page when you need paid platform services. Check the purchase result in Credits & Earnings before spending your credits.

You do not need a Zcash wallet to browse or publish. A wallet is needed only if you choose a Zcash payment or direct Zcash support.

Start with [Tuzis, your platform usage credits](/getting-started/tuzis), when you want to try paid services. Creators can also learn about [earnings for qualifying delivered services](/revenue-sharing/).
