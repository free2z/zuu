# Meet Tuzis: your Free2Z usage credits

2Zs—pronounced “Tuzis”—keep things moving on Free2Z. Use them to try AI tools, join a livestream, subscribe to a creator, comment, vote, boost a page, or use a supported SDK application. Holding more credits also unlocks larger uploads.

## Buy, use, share

| What you want to do | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Get credits | Open **Credits & Earnings** and choose an available card or Zcash purchase option |
| Use a service | Check its displayed price and your available credits |
| Help another user | Open their profile or page and choose **Support → 2Z** |
| Join a membership | Review the creator’s monthly credit price and access benefits |

Credits are for services inside Free2Z. You can share them, but you cannot cash them out or redeem a balance for Zcash. Buying or receiving credits does not earn creator compensation. They are usage credits, not an investment or an asset bought for appreciation.

## What does it cost?

The app shows each service’s price. AI costs depend on the model and request; livestreams, subscriptions, comments, votes, and boosts have their own prices. Your credit balance determines your upload size limit. Review the amount before confirming.

Starting a livestream requires at least **150 2Zs**. Applying for creator verification has no minimum credit balance.

## Buying with Zcash

Credits & Earnings uses a conservative **10% estimate buffer** to help you choose how much Zcash to send. You will likely receive more credits than the estimate, but the buffer is not a deduction or a guaranteed minimum: market prices can move. The final credits depend on the actual confirmed Zcash received and the fresh market price when Free2Z processes it; the estimate does not reserve a rate.

**Free2Z currently charges no Zcash purchase fee or spread.** Review the purchase details, then copy Credits & Earnings’s destination and memo into your wallet. Your wallet’s network fee is separate.

After sending, wait for confirmations and check the purchase result in Credits & Earnings. Don’t send again just because credits have not appeared. Keep the transaction reference if you need help.

For any bonus offers displayed in Credits & Earnings, check the terms: the highest eligible offer active when the confirmed payment is processed applies, without stacking. Unknown or expired codes do not prevent normal credits.

[Follow the buying guide](/guides/money/buy-2z).

## Creator earnings

A verified creator can earn USD for qualifying delivered pay-per-view livestreams or SDK services under their accepted agreement and service terms. Credits & Earnings keeps those earnings separate from credits and shows the amount owed, reserved for payment, and available to request.

[Learn how creator earnings work](/revenue-sharing/).

## Direct Zcash support

You can also send Zcash from your wallet to a creator’s displayed support address. That is a direct wallet transfer. Check the recipient and your wallet’s privacy settings before sending.
