# Sharing 2Z usage credits

Help a creator keep making things. Share 2Zs for their next AI request, livestream, or other Free2Z activity. Shared credits are theirs to use on the platform; they have no cash-out option.

## Buy usage credits

1. Sign in and choose **Credits** from your account menu.
2. Choose the card or Zcash purchase method offered by the app.
3. Review the current amount, pricing, fees, and payment instructions before submitting.
4. Check the actual purchase status. A payment being received is separate from credits being issued. If a purchase needs resolution, contact support with its reference; do not assume the credits are available.

For a Zcash purchase, use the exact current address, amount, and memo supplied by the app. Do not reuse an address, numeric exchange example, or QR code from an old screenshot.

## Share usage credits

1. Open the intended creator profile or zPage and select **Support**.
2. Choose the **2Z** tab and review the recipient and credit quantity.
3. Submit once. If the outcome is unknown, retry the original request rather than starting a second transfer.
4. Check the actual result and your current balance.

A subscription uses usage credits for access to the creator's services under its displayed price and renewal terms. Votes, comments, and boosts also consume usage credits according to the price shown in the app.

## Other kinds of support

The **Zcash** tab sends direct support from your own wallet to the displayed profile or page address. This is distinct from a company payment of recorded USD earnings. Check the destination and wallet fees before sending.
