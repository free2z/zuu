# Watch a livestream

Catch a performance, learn something new, or join the conversation. Open a creator’s profile or shared live link and check the access type before joining.

- A public broadcast may allow watching without an account.
- Subscriber-only rooms require the creator's membership.
- Pay-per-view shows its usage-credit price. Sign in and check your available credits before joining.
- Private rooms require the invitation link supplied by the host.

Use the live room's camera, microphone, chat, and leave controls as needed. Only grant device permissions you want to use. Your credits pay for the room’s displayed access price.
