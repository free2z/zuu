# Explore on your terms

Come for a story, stay for a conversation. You can read public zPages, explore profiles, and watch open broadcasts without signing up. **We will never sell your personal data.**

## Start without an account

- Read public pages and comments.
- Discover creators and join open broadcast livestreams.
- Send direct Zcash support to a creator who shares a receiving address.

## Join when you’re ready

Choose a username, password, and complete the signup check. Email is optional; adding one helps with account recovery. Your bio is yours to write, and you don’t need a bank account to publish or take part.

An account lets you comment, share credits, subscribe, and publish your own work.

## Keep your choices private

Use a wallet you control and choose shielded Zcash transactions when your wallet and the receiving address support them. Put only what you want the recipient to read in a memo. Think about what your public profile, posts, and reused usernames reveal about you.

External embeds and wallet services have their own privacy practices. Choose which services you trust and check your wallet’s transaction details before sending.
