# Support creators with Zcash

Send a little appreciation straight to a creator’s wallet. Zcash makes direct support possible without a Free2Z account, and shielded transactions keep payment details off the public chain when the wallets and addresses support them.

## Set up your receiving address

1. Open a Zcash wallet you control and copy a receiving address.
2. Add it to your Free2Z profile’s Zcash address field.
3. Use a page-specific address when you want support for a zPage to reach a different wallet.

## Send support

1. Open **Support → Zcash** on a creator’s profile or zPage.
2. Enter an amount and, when the receiving address supports memos, add or edit your message. A page can include a suggested message; make it your own.
3. Open your wallet using the payment link, scan the QR code, or copy the address.
4. Check the recipient, amount, message, and network fee in your wallet before sending. Wallets vary in how they handle payment links and memos.

Your message is for the recipient. Share only what you want them to read. For shielded privacy, check that your wallet sends to the shielded receiver you intend.

Free2Z displays the creator’s address; the direct transfer goes from your wallet to theirs.

## Find a wallet

Explore the official [Zcash wallet directory](https://z.cash/wallets/).

## Zcash and 2Zs work together

Send Zcash for direct financial support. Share 2Zs to help someone use Free2Z: AI, subscriptions, boosts, comments, and livestream time.
