Check each feature’s price, access requirements, and limits before using it. Keep your account credentials secure and choose carefully what you publish.

## Credits and creator services

[Usage credits](/legal/usage-credits) pay for platform services and supported access. They have no cash-out option.

For qualifying creator services, read and accept the [creator compensation agreement and service terms](/legal/creator-compensation). They describe the contribution you supply, your delivery duties, and the USD compensation the company owes. Completing tax information or holding credits is separate from accepting that agreement.

Applicable law protects required refunds and other nonwaivable rights.
