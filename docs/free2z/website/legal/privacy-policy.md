# Privacy Policy

Choose what you share publicly and understand what each feature needs. This page summarizes the main privacy considerations; read the [full Privacy Policy](https://free2z.com/docs/legal/privacy-policy) for data categories, processors, retention, and your rights.

## Accounts and public content

An email address is optional. You can browse public pages without an account. Published pages, profiles, and public upload links can be seen by others, so avoid posting identifying details or payment documents there.

**We will never sell your personal data.** Different features need different information: a public profile, creator verification, and payment review serve separate purposes.

## Zcash and wallets

Direct Zcash support goes to the displayed receiving address. Transaction privacy depends on the wallet and address type. Read the [Zcash privacy guide](/privacy/zcash) before choosing how to send.

## Creator verification and payment records

The company uses the information reasonably needed to verify payee identity and authority, administer the agreement, review lawful payment, and substantiate delivered services and amounts owed. Record retention depends on the record’s purpose and applicable duties.

For access, correction, deletion, or a question about retention, contact [help@free2z.com](mailto:help@free2z.com). The full privacy notice explains applicable rights and how to exercise them.
