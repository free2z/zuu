# Usage credits and purchases

This is a plain-language guide to buying and using 2Zs. Review the purchase terms displayed in Credits & Earnings before paying, and read the [purchase and refund policy](https://free2z.com/docs/legal/token-sale-and-usage-terms) for more information. Review the Zcash purchase details and terms in Credits & Earnings before sending.

## What you buy

2Zs, also called Tuzis, are credits for Free2Z services and supported access. Use them for AI, livestreams, comments, votes, boosts, memberships, and supported SDK applications. Your held credit balance also determines your upload size limit. You can share credits with another user within the platform’s limits.

Credits are not an investment or company equity. Holding or receiving them does not earn USD compensation, and a balance has no cash-out option. Creator compensation for qualifying delivered services is covered by a [separate agreement](/legal/creator-compensation). Buying platform usage credits is an independent purchase; it does not redeem credits for creator compensation.

## Review your purchase

Review your quantity, price or estimate, payment method, and fees or spread in Credits & Earnings. Check the result for any amount applied to an outstanding usage charge. A received payment and an applied credit purchase are separate steps. If delivery needs resolution, keep the original reference and contact support.

Purchase limits account for balances, incoming credits, and outstanding purchases. Spending credits does not immediately replenish the day’s loading allowance. The app checks its limits before accepting a purchase.

## Zcash payment instructions

The Zcash amount and credit quantity displayed before payment are estimates, not a reserved exchange rate. Credits & Earnings uses a conservative **10% estimate buffer**, so you will likely receive more credits than the estimate. The buffer is not a deduction or a guaranteed minimum; market prices can move before processing.

Free2Z calculates the credits from the actual Zcash received after confirmation using a fresh market price when it processes the payment. **Free2Z currently charges no Zcash purchase fee or spread.** Your wallet’s network fee is separate.

Copy the destination and memo displayed in Credits & Earnings. The memo helps match the payment to your account; it does not identify a shielded sender. After sending, wait for confirmations and the purchase result rather than paying again. Keep the transaction reference and ask support about a missing or mismatched payment.

If Credits & Earnings shows a bonus offer, read its terms before paying. An offer must be active and eligible when your confirmed payment is processed. The highest eligible offer applies; offers do not stack. An unknown or expired code does not prevent normal credits or another eligible offer.
