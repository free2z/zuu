# Creator compensation: the essentials

This page is a plain-language summary. **Read the full creator agreement and service terms displayed in Credits & Earnings before accepting them.** Those documents govern the service you supply and the payment you receive.

## What the company pays for

2Z Inc. supplies qualifying platform services to customers. A participating creator supplies the licensed livestream performance or application contribution described in the relevant service terms. The company owes compensation in USD for qualifying delivery under those accepted terms.

Paid livestream PPV and approved SDK contributions are the covered activities. Credits shared as support, subscriptions, and memberships provide platform use and access; they do not earn USD compensation.

## What you agree to do

Provide accurate payee and authority information, hold the rights and permissions for your contribution, deliver the service you offer, and provide the information reasonably needed for lawful payment. Read the service terms for its delivery, completion, cancellation, refund, and compensation rules before offering it.

Payee verification and completing tax information are separate from accepting the compensation agreement. Your acceptance covers the full agreement and displayed service terms.

## Earnings and payments

Earnings are company-owed USD recorded separately from platform credits. The amount depends on the qualifying delivered service and its collected allocation. It is not calculated by cashing out a credit balance.

The ordinary due date is 30 calendar days after verified service settlement unless the service terms provide an earlier date, subject to lawful holds. You can request any positive available amount. The review target is seven calendar days after required payee, tax, and address information is complete; this is not a guaranteed payment date.

The company pays to your authorized shielded Zcash address using an external market spot rate no more than five minutes old when it signs. It charges no exchange-rate spread and covers network fees and upward rounding. A payment request reserves the amount; only a confirmed payment settles it. An uncertain outcome requires reconciliation before another payment.

Ending participation or losing eligibility for further services does not forfeit compensation already owed. Refunds, corrections, and any recovery require an identifiable basis under the agreement or law. Nothing in this summary excludes mandatory consumer remedies or other nonwaivable rights.
