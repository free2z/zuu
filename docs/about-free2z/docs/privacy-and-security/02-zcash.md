# Zcash on Free2Z

Zcash serves three different purposes on Free2Z. Choose the guide for what you want to do.

| Action | Where the payment goes |
| --- | --- |
| [Buy 2Z credits](../getting-started/03-tuzis.md) | To the company using the purchase instructions in Credits & Earnings |
| [Send direct support](../for-supporters/donating-with-zcash.md) | From your wallet to the creator's displayed wallet address |
| [Receive creator earnings](../revenue-sharing/index.md) | From 2Z Inc. to your authorized shielded address to pay recorded USD earnings |

## Before using your wallet

Check the destination, amount, network fee, and memo. A credit purchase requires the memo displayed in Credits & Earnings. Copy it as shown rather than replacing it with a personal message. Follow the payment instructions shown on that page.

Wallets and address types provide different privacy controls. Review your wallet's documentation to understand what it reveals and what the recipient can read. Keep your wallet keys and recovery phrase private.

To receive direct support, add an address for a wallet you control in your profile settings. You do not need to hand Free2Z your wallet keys.
