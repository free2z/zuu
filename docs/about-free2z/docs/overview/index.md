# Welcome to Free2Z

Write something worth sharing. Go live with your community. Build an application people can use. Free2Z brings publishing, AI, livestreams, and Zcash together in one place.

You can explore public pages and profiles without an account. [Create an account](../getting-started/01-quickstart.md) when you want to publish, join paid experiences, or use account tools.

## Buy, use, and share 2Zs

**2Zs, also called Tuzis, are platform credits.** Buy them with Zcash in Credits & Earnings, then use them for AI, livestream time and access, comments, votes, boosts, subscriptions, and supported SDK applications. Holding credits also unlocks larger uploads. You can also share credits with another user.

2Zs are not money and cannot be cashed out. [Learn how credits work](../getting-started/03-tuzis.md).

## Create something of your own

- [Publish a zPage](../for-creators/02-creating-a-zpage.md): write an article, add media, and choose who can read it.
- [Host a livestream](../for-creators/03-free2z-live.md): invite viewers to a public, subscriber, private, or pay-per-view room.
- [Build an SDK application](../for-creators/sdk-applications.md): let users authorize your application to use their credits within spending limits.

## Earn from qualifying services

Verified creators can earn money from 2Z Inc. for qualifying delivered pay-per-view livestream and SDK services under accepted agreements and service terms. Earnings are recorded in USD and paid in shielded Zcash. They are separate from your credits. [Set up creator earnings](../revenue-sharing/index.md).

Want to support someone directly? [Send Zcash to their displayed wallet address](../for-supporters/donating-with-zcash.md). That is a separate wallet-to-wallet transfer.
