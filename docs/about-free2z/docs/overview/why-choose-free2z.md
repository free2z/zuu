# Why Free2Z?

## A home for your work

Publish a short update, a detailed article, or a page full of media. Your profile brings your work together so people can find you and keep reading.

## More ways to create

Use AI tools to develop an idea, host a live conversation, or build an application with the SDK. [Explore the creator guides](../for-creators/index.md).

## Credits with a clear purpose

Buy 2Zs with Zcash and use them for platform services. Share credits, join memberships, or boost a page you enjoy. Choose the experiences that interest you, with prices shown before paid actions.

## Earnings you can follow

Qualifying delivered PPV and SDK services can earn USD owed by 2Z Inc. for verified creators with accepted service terms. Credits & Earnings keeps those earnings separate from credits and shows the status of shielded Zcash payments. [Learn about creator earnings](../revenue-sharing/index.md).

## Direct Zcash support

Creators can display their own Zcash address. Supporters send from their wallet directly to that address, with privacy controls provided by their wallet. [Learn how direct support works](../for-supporters/donating-with-zcash.md).

## Your choices matter

We will never sell your personal data. An email is optional when creating an account. Choose what you make public, keep your password safe, and understand the [privacy settings and limits](../privacy-and-security/index.md) of the tools you use.
