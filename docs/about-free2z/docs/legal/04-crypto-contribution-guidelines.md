---
title: Direct Zcash Tips
---

A creator can share a wallet address so you can send Zcash directly from your wallet. Free2Z shares the address; it does not receive or route that direct transfer. Check the recipient and address carefully before sending.

Direct wallet tips are different from sharing [2Z credits](./05-token-sale-and-usage-terms.md) on Free2Z. They also do not create a payment obligation from 2Z Inc. under the [creator agreement](./06-revenue-share-agreement.md).

## Your responsibilities

Use an address you control when requesting a direct tip. When sending, confirm the address and amount with the intended recipient. Protect your wallet and recovery information, and follow the laws and tax rules that apply to you.

Fraud, scams, money laundering, terrorism financing, and other unlawful uses are prohibited. Do not use another person's identity or misrepresent who receives a payment.

## Problems with a transfer

Free2Z cannot reverse a transfer between independent wallets. Contact the recipient if you sent the wrong amount or need to discuss a refund. For an address or account problem on Free2Z, contact [help@free2z.com](mailto:help@free2z.com).

These guidelines do not limit mandatory legal rights. Company-supplied platform purchases and creator payments are covered by their own terms and obligations.
