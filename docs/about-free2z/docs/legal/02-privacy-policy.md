## Introduction

Welcome to Free2Z. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and when necessary, disclose your personal information. This notice describes data processing; it does not create blanket consent to every purpose or substitute for a separately required choice.

## Scope

This policy applies to our website [https://free2z.com](https://free2z.com) and [https://free2z.cash](https://free2z.cash), encompassing all products and services provided by Free2Z.

It also applies to our mobile applications, which are distributed under the
identifiers `cash.free2z.zuuli` (ZUULI, the Zcash wallet), `cash.free2z.free2z`
(Free2Z, the content application) and `cash.free2z.e2e2z` (E2E2Z, the messaging
application). The applications have different roles. The data processed depends
on the features you use, including any Free2Z account you use across them.

## Data We Collect

**Personal Information**: Information you provide directly, such as your email address when creating an account or contacting us.

**Account Information**: Your username, the credentials you use to sign in, and
any third-party accounts you choose to link to your Free2Z account.

**Content You Create**: The zPages, comments, profile details, images and other
media you publish or upload. Content you publish is visible to others according
to the settings you choose.

**Identity Verification and Tax Documents**: If you apply to our creator earnings
programme, we collect the identity documents and tax form information you
submit for that application, a photograph of your face for comparison with
your identity document, and any signature you provide on a tax form.
See *Identity Verification and Tax Documents* below.

**Payment and Transaction Information**: Records of purchases you make, and your
2Z credit balance and its history. Card details are collected and processed by
our payment processor rather than by us.

**AI Processing**: Prompts and conversations, content processed for language
detection and search indexing, and queries submitted to semantic search.
See *AI Features* below.

**Livestream Content**: Video, audio and text shared during a livestream. See
*Livestreaming Features* below.

**Zcash Addresses**: Addresses you provide for account linking, signing in,
receiving support or receiving creator payments, and associated transaction
records. ZUULI stores its wallet recovery phrase and spending keys on your
device; it does not send them to Free2Z servers.

**Usage Data**: Automatically collected data about your interactions with our services, including IP addresses, browser types, and pages visited.

**Cookies**: Small data files used to enhance your user experience.

### **Identity Verification and Tax Documents**

Applying to our creator earnings programme is optional, and only creators
who choose to apply are asked for these materials. The application collects
government-issued identity documents, a photograph of your face to compare
with your identity document, and tax form information, including a signature
on the tax form, to assess your identity and eligibility and
administer creator payments.

We use this information to assess the application and administer the programme.
The sharing and legal-disclosure provisions above apply to these records.
Browsing, publishing and supporting creators do not require you to submit
these application materials.

### **Payments**

Card payments are collected and processed by our third-party payment processor.
We do not receive or store your full card number. We receive and retain the
transaction records associated with your account, including what was purchased
and your resulting 2Z credit balance.

Zcash transfers are recorded on the Zcash network. Transfers you initiate from
ZUULI use the wallet on your device; creator payments and other service-side
transactions may be sent by Free2Z. This does not give Free2Z access to your
ZUULI wallet recovery phrase or spending keys.

### **AI Features**

When you use our AI features, the prompts you submit and the resulting
conversations are processed by third-party model providers acting as our service
providers in order to generate a response. Model providers also process text
for automatic features: page titles and content, including unpublished drafts,
may be sent for language detection; content may be processed for search
indexing; and semantic search queries are sent to an embedding provider.
These uses can occur without opening AI chat or submitting an AI prompt.
Do not submit text to these features that you would not want processed in
this way.

### **Third-Party Apps**

You can connect your Free2Z account to apps made by other developers through the Free2Z SDK. Every app receives a stable account identifier, which is the same for every app, and when and how you last signed in. Beyond that, an app receives only what you approve on the consent screen. If you allow it, an app can receive your username, display name and avatar; your email address, if you have one; and your 2Z balance. With your permission, it can also stay signed in while you are not using it; start a card checkout to add 2Z credits, which you complete yourself on our payment processor's checkout page, and see the status of those requests; and use AI features that spend your 2Z credits. Accounts without an email address are welcome; their activity is not anonymous. You can remove an app's access at any time in Connected apps. The app developer's privacy notice describes what the app collects and does with information it receives.

When an app uses AI features through Free2Z, the request content is processed by Free2Z and sent to a third-party AI model provider to generate a response. The gateway does not add your Free2Z account identity to that provider request. Content supplied by an app can itself identify a person; omitting account fields does not make it anonymous. The gateway does not persist prompt and response content as part of its call records. This is different from information deliberately submitted in a support or safety report, which we process to investigate the report.

We keep account-linked and app-linked call records for metering, receipts, security and retry handling. These can include the model, usage quantities, charges, outcome, timestamps, request fingerprints, retry identifiers, schema names and app-supplied metadata. These records are linked to an account and an app and are not anonymous. The app can read its own call records for 90 days; that access window is not a promise to delete all records after 90 days. Billing, security and other records are retained for their purposes and applicable obligations as described under *Retention and security*. Network and browser information can also be processed to deliver and protect the service, including on hosted account pages.

Our AI model providers' API terms generally exclude training on API requests by default. No training, disabling provider response storage, and zero data retention are different controls. Provider retention varies by provider, feature and account configuration. Ordinary abuse-monitoring retention can be up to 30 days; providers can retain data longer for safety investigations, policy enforcement or legal obligations. For example, Anthropic describes retaining inputs and outputs flagged for potential policy violations for up to two years. See OpenAI's data controls at [developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) and Anthropic's retention policy at [privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data). Free2Z does not promise zero data retention across the SDK or its providers. Even an approved provider zero-retention arrangement can have feature-specific restrictions and safety or legal exceptions.

## Children's Privacy

Children under 13 may not hold Free2Z accounts or use Free2Z directly, including its website and its publishing, social, livestreaming and AI tools. An adult can connect their own account for a child's use of constrained features inside a third-party app under the [SDK developer requirements in our Terms](./01-terms-of-service-agreement.md#sdk-developer-requirements-for-apps-used-by-children). Those requirements also cover older minors where applicable law requires the relevant child protections or parental authorization.

The permitted arrangement uses app-generated requests from controlled content and bounded choices, such as a curriculum topic and a correct/incorrect result. Developers must enable the child-directed setting and prevent free-form prompts, uploads, child identifiers and individual histories from being sent to Free2Z. These are requirements for developers, not a claim that Free2Z automatically detects every prohibited input. Free2Z processes the request and retains the account-linked records described under *Third-Party Apps*. When a child uses such an app, the device's network information, such as its IP address, also reaches Free2Z when the app contacts our servers; we process it to deliver and protect the service. Even constrained choices or performance information may concern a child when associated with an account; the adult's ownership of the account does not change whose information it is.

The adult controls account access and spending. Account authorization is not age verification or legally required parental consent. The developer must provide notices and obtain any required permissions for its app; Free2Z is responsible for its own processing. Adult ownership, no-email accounts and provider retention settings do not automatically exempt either party from children's privacy requirements.

Do not send a child's personal details in prompts, metadata, uploads, reports or support messages. If you believe prohibited information has reached Free2Z, contact help@free2z.com with the app name and, if available, a call or report reference. Do not repeat the child's information in the initial message. We will investigate, restrict affected processing where appropriate, and delete prohibited information under our control where required. We will seek deletion from relevant service providers where applicable. Required legal preservation and limited security or transaction records may prevent immediate removal of every record; we will explain applicable limits when responding. Account deletion alone does not erase every provider copy, report or legally retained record.
