# Add images and files

Bring your pages to life with supported images and media. Your credit balance determines the upload size you can use. Holding credits unlocks larger uploads; check the size and format limits shown in your account.

1. Open the upload controls in your account or editor.
2. Choose a file you have the right to publish.
3. Review its size, format, and the displayed upload limits.
4. Wait for the upload to finish, then copy or insert its link.
5. Preview your page to check that the media appears as you intend.

Treat a public upload link as public, even if you have not yet added it to a page. Keep identity documents, wallet keys, and payment evidence out of public media. Use the verification or support instructions for sensitive documents.
