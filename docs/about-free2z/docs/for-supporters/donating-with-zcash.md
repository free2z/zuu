# Send direct Zcash support

Support a creator by sending Zcash from your wallet to their displayed address. The recipient receives the transfer in their own wallet; you do not need to buy 2Zs first.

1. Open the creator's profile or page and choose the Zcash support option.
2. Check that the displayed address is the recipient you intend to support.
3. Open your wallet or copy the payment details. Review the amount, address, and any memo before sending.
4. Check your wallet's network fee and privacy settings.
5. Keep the transaction record if you need to discuss the transfer with the recipient.

Direct support is separate from [sharing 2Z credits](./donating-with-2Zs.md). It does not add credits to an account or use the company payment form for USD earnings.

Creators can add a support address in profile settings. Use an address for a wallet you control; your public support address and your company earnings payment address serve different purposes.
