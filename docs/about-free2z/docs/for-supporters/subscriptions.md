# Join a creator's membership

Subscribe with 2Z credits for the benefits a creator offers, such as subscriber-only pages or livestream access.

1. Open the creator's profile and choose **Subscribe**.
2. Review the monthly price, offered benefits, and your credit balance.
3. Check the maximum price you authorize and the renewal details shown.
4. Confirm if you want to join.
5. Manage your price limit or cancel renewal from **Credits & Earnings**. Check the displayed renewal and access dates.

Creators set their membership price in profile settings. Read the offer before joining: benefits depend on what the creator provides. Subscriptions use and share credits; they do not create USD creator earnings.
