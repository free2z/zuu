# Share 2Zs

Give another user credits for the services they use on Free2Z. They can use those credits for AI, livestream services, and other platform actions. Holding credits also unlocks larger uploads.

## Get credits to share {#buying-2zs}

Choose **Credits** from your account menu and [buy 2Zs with Zcash](../getting-started/03-tuzis.md). Review the purchase instructions and check your available balance after the payment is processed. If card checkout is offered, check its exact charge before continuing.

### Buy with Zcash {#buy-2zs-with-zcash}

Follow the [Zcash purchase steps](../getting-started/03-tuzis.md#buy-with-zcash) in Credits & Earnings before using your wallet.

## Choose a recipient

1. Open the creator's profile or zPage and choose **Support**.
2. Choose the **2Z** tab and check the recipient and quantity.
3. Confirm once.
4. Check the result and your balance. If the outcome is unclear, keep the original request and check its status before sending again.

Sharing is subject to the limits shown in the app. Shared 2Zs are platform credits: they are not money, cannot be cashed out, and do not create USD earnings.

## Other ways to support

- [Subscribe](./subscriptions.md) with credits for the creator's membership benefits.
- [Send Zcash directly](./donating-with-zcash.md) from your wallet using the **Zcash** tab and the creator's displayed address.

Direct Zcash support is a wallet transfer, not a credit purchase or a company payment of creator earnings.
