# Get started

Welcome! Start with a page to read, an idea to publish, or a creator you want to follow.

1. [Create your account](./01-quickstart.md).
2. [Make your profile yours](./02-profile.md).
3. [Publish your first zPage](./04-zpages.md).
4. [Buy and use 2Z credits](./03-tuzis.md) when you want paid tools or access.

You can browse public content without an account. You do not need a Zcash wallet to read or publish; use one when you choose a Zcash purchase or direct support.

## Where next?

- [Create and publish](../for-creators/index.md): AI, uploads, livestreams, and SDK applications.
- [Use and share](../for-supporters/index.md): memberships, live access, credits, and direct Zcash support.
- [Creator earnings](../revenue-sharing/index.md): qualifying services, verification, and shielded Zcash payments.
