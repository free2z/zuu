# Make your profile yours

Your profile brings your published pages, biography, and membership offer together. Add a name and image people can recognize, and choose what you want to share.

1. Sign in and open profile settings from your dashboard.
2. Edit your display name and biography.
3. Add an avatar or header image if you want one.
4. Set a membership price in 2Zs if you offer subscriber benefits.
5. Save and open your public profile to check the result.

You can add your own Zcash address for [direct wallet support](../for-supporters/donating-with-zcash.md). Check that the address belongs to a wallet you control. It serves a different purpose from the shielded address you submit for company earnings payments.

Memberships use and share credits for access benefits. [Creator USD earnings](../revenue-sharing/index.md) come from qualifying PPV and SDK services.
