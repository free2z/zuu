# Your first few minutes

## Create an account

Open [Free2Z](https://free2z.com/) and choose **Sign In**, then the signup option. Pick a username and password, and complete the verification shown on screen.

An email is optional and gives you an email recovery option. Save your password securely before continuing.

Your new account starts with **10 2Zs**, a one-time welcome grant of platform credits, so you can post, comment and vote right away. [Learn about 2Zs](./03-tuzis.md).

## Introduce yourself

Open profile settings from your dashboard. Add a display name, a short biography, and an image if you want one. Preview your public profile to see what others see.

## Publish something

Choose **Write**, add a title and your content, and preview your page. Select **Publish** when it is ready. You can return through My Pages to edit it.

## Try more with credits

Choose **Credits** from your account menu to open **Credits & Earnings**. Buy 2Zs with Zcash for AI tools, livestream services, and other paid actions. Holding credits also unlocks larger uploads. After the payment is processed, check the credits available to use.

[Understand 2Zs](./03-tuzis.md) before buying, or [explore creator tools](../for-creators/index.md).
