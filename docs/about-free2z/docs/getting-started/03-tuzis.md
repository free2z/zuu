# 2Zs: credits for creating and connecting

**2Zs, also called Tuzis, are platform credits.** Buy them with Zcash, use them on Free2Z, or share them with another user.

**New accounts start with 10 2Zs**, a welcome grant from Free2Z added once when you sign up with a username and password or with Google, X or GitHub. Accounts created by signing in with a Zcash wallet start at zero. Like all 2Zs they are platform credits, not money, and cannot be cashed out.

Use credits for AI, livestream time and access, comments, votes, boosts, subscriptions, and supported SDK applications. Holding credits also unlocks larger uploads. Each paid action shows its price or spending controls in the app.

## Buy with Zcash

1. Sign in and open **Credits** from your account menu.
2. Choose Zcash and review the estimated credits, ZEC amount, and rate.
3. Read the purchase terms and check the receiving address and payment memo shown on the page.
4. Use a shielded Zcash wallet and follow the displayed payment instructions for the amount, address, and memo. Your wallet's network fee is separate.
5. Return to Credits & Earnings to check your credit balance.

The Zcash estimate includes a conservative **10% buffer**, so you’ll likely receive more credits. Price changes mean the estimate is not a fixed quote or a guaranteed minimum.

Free2Z currently charges **no purchase fee or spread** on Zcash payments. After three confirmations, credits are calculated from the Zcash actually received at the market price used for processing. Your wallet’s network fee is separate.

If the purchase page shows a Zcash bonus offer, check its terms before paying. The offer must be active when your confirmed payment is processed. The highest eligible offer applies; offers do not stack. An expired or unrecognized code does not stop a valid payment: you still receive ordinary credits or an eligible platform-wide offer.

The memo helps Free2Z match your payment. Copy it from the payment instructions rather than typing it from memory. Follow the payment instructions shown on the page.

A detected payment may still be processing. Check your credit balance in Credits & Earnings after the payment is processed before spending the credits. If a payment is delayed or needs help, keep its reference and contact [help@free2z.com](mailto:help@free2z.com); do not send a second payment just because the first is still pending.

If the purchase page offers card checkout, review its exact charge and quantity before continuing to Stripe. [Purchase and refund terms](/docs/legal/token-sale-and-usage-terms/) apply to credit purchases.

## Follow credits and purchases

Open **Credits** from your account menu to reach **Credits & Earnings**. Your credit balance shows what you can use; creator USD earnings have their own panel.

**Credit activity** brings together your subscriptions, subscribers, and card purchases. **Card purchases** lists Stripe purchases, not every movement of credits. Zcash purchases are not listed there. After Free2Z processes a Zcash payment, check your credit balance and keep the payment reference if you need help.

## Use and share

Check your balance in Credits & Earnings. Choose a service, review its price, and use your credits. AI costs depend on the selected model and request; membership and pay-per-view prices are set by the creator and shown in the app.

To give someone credits, open their profile or page, choose **Support**, and select the **2Z** tab. Review the recipient and amount before confirming the share. [Learn about sharing](../for-supporters/donating-with-2Zs.md).

Starting a livestream requires at least **150 2Zs** in your usage balance. You do not need that balance to apply for creator verification.

## Credits and earnings are different

| Balance | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| 2Z credits | Buying and sharing access to platform services |
| USD earnings | Money owed by 2Z Inc. for qualifying delivered PPV or SDK services under accepted creator terms |

**2Zs are not money and cannot be cashed out.** Buying, holding, sharing, or receiving credits does not earn USD. Subscriptions use credits for membership benefits.

Verified creators can earn separately from qualifying delivered services, track those earnings in USD, and request payment in shielded Zcash. [Learn how creator earnings work](../revenue-sharing/index.md).

Direct Zcash support is another option: it goes from a supporter's wallet to the address displayed by the creator. It does not add 2Z credits or use the creator earnings payment form.
