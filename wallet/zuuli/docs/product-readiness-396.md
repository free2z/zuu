## Surface evidence

On this checkout both apps passed `npm run typecheck` and `npm run typecheck:tests`. The focused Zuuli Vitest selection (`SocialButtons`, `social-providers`, `http`, `donation-http`, `card-checkout`, `native-return`) passed 94 tests; `zec-top-up-demo`, funding `lib` and `send-recipient` passed 45. The Free2Z selection (`SocialButtons`, `social-providers`, `http`, `donation-http`, `subscription-contract`, `live-ppv-contract`, `live-join-contract`, `live-ticket`, `Stage`) passed 84. These are controlled boundary tests, including refusal, idempotency, retry and parsing; they do not perform a production transaction. Zuuli's social discovery browser suite passed at 320 and 360 pixels, and Free2Z's profile/KYC browser suite passed nine tests, using mock mode. Full Playwright suites started concurrently but timed out at page navigation under local load and were stopped; the focused suites were rerun one worker at a time.
