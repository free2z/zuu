What e2e2z is

  • Private messages for people on free2z.
  • Messages are encrypted on your phone before they are sent. free2z's servers pass them along but can't read them.
  • Opening this link never sends a message. You decide whether to reply.

e2e2z is in early internal testing and hasn't been independently audited. During testing, free2z also runs the directory that publishes each person's encryption keys, with no independent check yet, and that directory will be reset before a public launch.
